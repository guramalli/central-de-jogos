Professor Coral, the collector

Atlantis

Hello, young star! I study EVERYTHING in the caves of Atlantis. Bring me what the opponents drop and I'll pay well for my collection!

Where to find

🗺️ Atlantis — Sunken City — in the middle of the map, near the sign “🔱 NEPTUNE SQUARE”

🧭 Trip: 🌊 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to Captain Iara (at the port) and go down by submarine — you need the Diving Helmet.

🧭 Path: the trip takes you right there.
