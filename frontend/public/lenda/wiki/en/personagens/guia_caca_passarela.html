Signora Valentina, the fashion designer

Milan

Milan is one of the fashion capitals of the world: designers show off new clothes on the runways here. Only Wing Stylist (level 162) shows up in this hunting area, and there are lots of them. Want a mission?

Where to find

🗺️ 🎯 Fashion Runway — embaixo of the map (south), near the sign “🎯 FASHION RUNWAY”

🧭 Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to Milan, Italy (from level 156) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.

🧭 Path: from there: Milan → 🎯 Fashion Runway
