Seu Nonato, the fisherman

Brazil

Did you know crabs walk sideways? That’s why they’re so hard to dribble past here in the grotto! Only Pinchy Crab (level 10) shows up in this hunting area, and there are lots of them. Want a mission?

🧭 Path: from Campinho Village: Campinho Village → Footvolley Beach → 🎯 Crab Grotto
