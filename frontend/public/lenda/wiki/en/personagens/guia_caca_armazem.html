Seu Odair, the dockworker

Santos

The port of Santos is the biggest in Brazil. For a long time, Brazilian coffee left from here for the whole world. Only Vila Belmiro Sheriff (level 188) shows up in this hunting area, and there are lots of them. Want a mission?
