Bike with a basket

Level 12 Mount/look Brazil

Given by: Seu Pedal, from the bike shop
is at Campinho Village — em cima of the map (north)

The story

I built a beautiful bike with a basket! To finish it, I need some things from the beach.

What to do

  • Collect 12x Pretty Seashell
  • Collect 15x Stolen Beach Ball
  • Bring 600 coins

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Pinchy Crab — Level 10 (drops the most Pretty Seashell)
🎒Each item: Pretty Seashell: Pinchy Crab (Level 10, Footvolley Beach)
Stolen Beach Ball: Thief Seagull (Level 10, Footvolley Beach)
🗺️Where: Footvolley Beach (Brazil)
🧭Path: from Campinho Village: Campinho Village → Footvolley Beach
📌Inside: em cima, on the right of the map (northeast), near the sign “🚣 FISHERMEN'S PIER”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 2,500 XP
  • 🛹 Mount: Bike with a Basket
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

It's yours! With it you'll fly along the boardwalk.

⬅️ First, finish: My first skateboard
