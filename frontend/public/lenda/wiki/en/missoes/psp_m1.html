🛂 The Star's Passport

Level 8 Side quest Brazil

Given by: Dona Zuleide, from the Travel Agency
is at Campinho Village — on the right of the map (east)

The story

Every star who travels learns a little about each place! This is your Star's Passport. Get my 3 questions right, here at the Campinho Village Travel Agency, and earn your first stamp.

What to do

🎯 Earn 1 stamp in the Star's Passport (3 questions from Dona Zuleide, in the Village)

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

🙋Look for: Dona Zuleide, from the Travel Agency (asks the 3 Passport questions)
🗺️Where: Campinho Village (Brazil)
📌Inside: on the right of the map (east)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 300 XP
  • 🪙 80 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

What a pretty stamp! In lots of places around the world, someone has questions for you. Look for the 🛂 button in conversations.

➡️ Then unlocks: 🛂 Stamps on the road
