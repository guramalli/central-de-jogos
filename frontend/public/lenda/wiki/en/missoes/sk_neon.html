Collection: Neon Samurai

Level 75 Mount/look Brazil

Given by: Dona Vera, the fashion designer
is at City — Futsal Courts — embaixo, on the left of the map (southwest)

The story

Tokyo lights and trophies from the Neon Arena. I want rare stuff!

What to do

  • Collect 40x Japanese Fan
  • Collect 30x Neon Shades
  • Collect 3x Neon Sakura Arena Trophy

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Sumo Wrestler — Level 80 (drops the most Japanese Fan)
🎒Each item: Japanese Fan: Sumo Wrestler (Level 80, Tokyo)
Neon Shades: Beach Bodybuilder (Level 93, Miami)
Neon Sakura Arena Trophy: Neon Ronin (Level 90, boss, Neon Sakura Arena)
🗺️Where: Tokyo — Neon District
🧭Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to Tokyo, Japan (from level 74) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: em cima, on the right of the map (northeast), near the sign “🥋 RYOGOKU”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 243,230 XP
  • 👕 New look: neon
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

So shiny! Put it on with the ✨ Look button.

⬅️ First, finish: Collection: Dribble Pharaoh

➡️ Then unlocks: Collection: Ice Star
