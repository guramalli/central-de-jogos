The Moon Captain

Level 326 Story Brazil

Given by: Commander Nova
is at Galactic Space Station — in the middle of the map

The story

The Moon Captain is the most famous star on the Moon. Beat him on his own turf!

What to do

  • Beat 1x Moon Captain

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Moon Captain boss — Level 328
🗺️Where: Moon — Sea of Tranquility
🧭Trip: 🚀 Go to the Space Station (in Rio, with Dr. Estela — you need the Astronaut Suit), and from there the Control Tower takes you to Moon.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: on the right of the map (east)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 37,265,570 XP
  • 🪙 2,500,000 coins
  • 1x Moon Amulet
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

The Moon is ours! Now Mars wants to play.

➡️ Then unlocks: The General of Mars
