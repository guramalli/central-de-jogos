💭 The memory album

Level 3 Side quest Brazil

Given by: Lia, the dreamer
is at Campinho Village — in the middle of the map

The story

I'm going to keep a memento from every place in the world! But I need to start right here... Can you bring me 5 Bottle Caps? The Village opponents are always dropping them.

What to do

🎯 Collect 5x Bottle Caps (Village)

  • Collect 5x Bottle Cap

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Cheeky Pigeon — Level 3 (drops the most Bottle Cap)
🗺️Where: Campinho Village (Brazil)
📌Inside: em cima of the map (north)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 66 XP
  • 🪙 150 coins
  • 1x Sticker Pack
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

First memento stuck in! Someday this album will have the whole world.
