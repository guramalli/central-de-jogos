The blue scooter

Level 30 Mount/look Brazil

Given by: Dona Rita from the Garage
is at City — Futsal Courts — embaixo, on the left of the map (southwest)

The story

I'm restoring a classic scooter. Bring me parts and the money for the paint job!

What to do

  • Collect 12x Skateboard Wheel
  • Collect 10x Training Cone
  • Collect 8x Piece of Leather
  • Bring 6,000 coins

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Cheeky Skater — Level 20 (drops the most Skateboard Wheel)
🎒Each item: Skateboard Wheel: Cheeky Skater (Level 20, City)
Training Cone: Futsal Pivot (Level 23, City)
Piece of Leather: Dirt Pitch Defender (Level 29, Dirt Pitch Arena)
🗺️Where: City — Futsal Courts (Brazil)
🧭Path: from Campinho Village: Campinho Village → Footvolley Beach → City
📌Inside: em cima, on the left of the map (northwest)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 16,279 XP
  • 🛹 Mount: Blue Scooter
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

Look how pretty! Go ahead and start it up.

➡️ Then unlocks: My first car
