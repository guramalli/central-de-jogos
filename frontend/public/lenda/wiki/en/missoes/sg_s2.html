📜 The fire that never goes out

Level 540 Origin Saga Saturn

Given by: Astronomer Vega
is at Saturn — Crystal Rings — in the middle of the map

The story

Bring 10 Eternal Embers. They come from Tower Chests, the prizes from the Infinite Tower bosses, and Master Altina trades chests for Tokens.

What to do

🎯 Collect 10 Eternal Embers (Tower Chests)

  • Collect 10x Eternal Ember

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Ember Golem — Level 432 (drops the most Eternal Ember)
🙋Also: Master Altina, of the Infinite Tower (trades Tower Tokens for Tower Chests — and a chest may hold Eternal Embers)
🗺️Where: 🎯 Lava River (🌀 Multiverse Stadium)
🧭Trip: 🌀 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to the Multiverse Guardian.
🧭Path: from there: 🌀 Multiverse Stadium → ⛏️ Kingdom of Stonehold → 🎯 Lava River
📌Inside: on the left of the map (west)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 154,417,019 XP
  • 🪙 3,000,000 coins
