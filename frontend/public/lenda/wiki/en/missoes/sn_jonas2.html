💭 The pharaohs' writing

Level 52 Side quest Brazil

Given by: Seu Jonas, the fisherman
is at Footvolley Beach — on the left of the map (west)

The story

If you cross this whole sea, you get to Egypt. If you go to Cairo, can you bring me an Ancient Papyrus? I want to see how the pharaohs wrote!

What to do

🎯 Collect 1x Ancient Papyrus (Cairo)

  • Collect 1x Ancient Papyrus

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Pyramid Runner — Level 56 (drops the most Ancient Papyrus)
🗺️Where: Cairo — On the Banks of the Nile
🧭Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to Cairo, Egypt (from level 50) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: on the left of the map (west)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 69,432 XP
  • 🪙 15,600 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

Little drawings instead of letters! The Egyptians wrote like this more than 4 thousand years ago.

⬅️ First, finish: 💭 The turtle nest

➡️ Then unlocks: 💭 The land of the explorers
