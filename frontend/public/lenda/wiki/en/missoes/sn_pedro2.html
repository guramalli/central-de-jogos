💭 The jumping fans

Level 112 Side quest Brazil

Given by: Pedrinho, young fan
is at Legendary Stadium — embaixo, on the right of the map (southeast)

The story

In Buenos Aires the fans jump the whole game, and then they dance the tango! Can you bring me a Tango Shoe from there?

What to do

🎯 Collect 1x Tango Shoe (Buenos Aires)

  • Collect 1x Tango Shoe

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Caminito Pibe — Level 106 (drops the most Tango Shoe)
🗺️Where: Buenos Aires — La Boca
🧭Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to Buenos Aires, Argentina (from level 100) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: on the right of the map (east), near the sign “🎨 CAMINITO”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 567,230 XP
  • 🪙 33,600 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

A tango shoe! I'll dance in the stands at the next goal.

➡️ Then unlocks: 💭 Rain in London
