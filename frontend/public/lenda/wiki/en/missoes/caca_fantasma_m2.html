Great Hunt: Ghost Stadium

Level 280 Side quest Atlantis

Given by: Seu Assombrado, the kit manager
is at 🎯 Ghost Stadium — embaixo of the map (south)

The story

Now the real challenge: get past 120 Little Ghost Fan opponents, here in the Ghost Stadium hunting area. Whoever finishes this becomes the area's Official Hunter!

What to do

  • Beat 120x Little Ghost Fan

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Little Ghost Fan — Level 282
🗺️Where: 🎯 Ghost Stadium (Atlantis)
🧭Trip: 🌊 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to Captain Iara (at the port) and go down by submarine — you need the Diving Helmet.
🧭Path: from there: Atlantis → 🎯 Ghost Stadium
📌Inside: on the left of the map (west)
