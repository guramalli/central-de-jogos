Peace Mission: Bombonerita

Level 102 Story Buenos Aires

Given by: Profe Martín
is at Buenos Aires — La Boca — em cima of the map (north)

The story

The fanatics of Bombonerita scare everyone who walks by. Beat 25 of them with fair play and show that rivalry belongs on the field.

What to do

  • Beat 25x Bombonerita Fan

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Bombonerita Fan — Level 106
🗺️Where: Buenos Aires — La Boca
🧭Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to Buenos Aires, Argentina (from level 100) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: em cima, on the right of the map (northeast)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 424,330 XP
  • 🪙 15,900 coins
  • 1x Peace Scarf
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

Today they sang instead of booing. Cheering is a party!

➡️ Then unlocks: El Maestro del Tango
