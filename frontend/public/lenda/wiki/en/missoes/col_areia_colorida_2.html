Big order: Little Bottle of Colored Sand

Level 207 Side quest Atlantis

Given by: Professor Coral, the collector
is at Atlantis — Sunken City — in the middle of the map

The story

I'm building a museum in Atlantis and I'm missing 25× Little Bottle of Colored Sand — it’s a big order, but the prize is worth it! Who drops it: Giant Sand Worm (Giant Worm Dunes).

What to do

  • Collect 25x Little Bottle of Colored Sand

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Giant Sand Worm — Level 210 (drops the most Little Bottle of Colored Sand)
🗺️Where: 🎯 Giant Worm Dunes (Atlantis)
🧭Trip: 🌊 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to Captain Iara (at the port) and go down by submarine — you need the Diving Helmet.
🧭Path: from there: Atlantis → 🎯 Giant Worm Dunes
📌Inside: on the right of the map (east)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 5,616,994 XP
  • 🪙 168,000 coins
  • 4x Gold Thread
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

Amazing! 25× Little Bottle of Colored Sand! Nobody has ever brought me this many. Take these gold threads to the Forge!

⬅️ First, finish: Collection: Little Bottle of Colored Sand
