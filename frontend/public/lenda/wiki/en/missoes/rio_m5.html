The World Cup

Level 192 Story Brazil

Given by: Dona Glória, national team coach
is at Rio de Janeiro — Copacabana — in the middle of the map

The story

The King gave you his blessing at Vila Belmiro. The time has come: the WORLD CUP ARENA has opened its doors here in Rio, and THE CUP LEGEND is waiting for you on the field. Win the final and bring the trophy home!

What to do

  • Beat 1x The Cup Legend

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: The Cup Legend boss — Level 200
🗺️Where: World Cup Arena (Brazil)
🧭Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to Rio de Janeiro, Brazil (from level 112) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.
🧭Path: from there: Rio de Janeiro → World Cup Arena

Rewards

  • ⭐ 5,208,052 XP
  • 🪙 2,000,000 coins
  • 1x World Cup Trophy

WORLD CHAMPION! From the little dirt pitch all the way to the most famous trophy on the planet. The whole Village is crying with joy!
