👑 King Rex

Level 695 Story 🌀 Multiverse Stadium

Given by: Dr. Fossil, the paleontologist
is at 🦖 Jurassic Valley — Camp — in the middle of the map

The story

King Rex wants to swallow every ball in the valley! When he roars, hide behind a rock; when red circles show up on the ground, step out of them. Beat King Rex in King Rex’s Arena!

King Rex doesn’t like soccer and wants to drive the humans out of the Jurassic Valley. Nobody has ever beaten him.

What to do

  • Beat 1x King Rex, the Ball Devourer

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: King Rex, the Ball Devourer boss — Level 700
🗺️Where: 👑 King Rex's Arena (🌀 Multiverse Stadium)
🧭Trip: 🌀 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to the Multiverse Guardian.
🧭Path: from there: 🌀 Multiverse Stadium → 🦖 Jurassic Valley → 🦖 Jurassic Maze → 👑 King Rex's Arena
📌Inside: in the middle of the map

Rewards

  • ⭐ 563,123,700 XP
  • 🪙 9,600,000 coins
  • 2x Tower Chest
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

YOU BEAT KING REX! The whole valley is celebrating... and the dinosaurs want to play soccer with you!
