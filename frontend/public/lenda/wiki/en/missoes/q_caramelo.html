Runaway Caramelo

Level 5 Story Brazil

Given by: Seu Zé
is at Campinho Village — embaixo, on the left of the map (southwest)

The story

The wild Caramelo dogs ran off with the balls from the little pitch! Dribble past them gently, here in the Village, and bring me back 2 Flat Balls.

What to do

🎯 Get back 2 Flat Balls from the Caramelo Dogs

  • Collect 2x Flat Ball (Caramelo the Dog)

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Caramelo the Dog — Level 6 (drops the most Flat Ball)
🗺️Where: Campinho Village (Brazil)
📌Inside: em cima, on the right of the map (northeast)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 320 XP
  • 🪙 60 coins
  • 1x Sweatband

They just wanted to play... Take this sweatband!
