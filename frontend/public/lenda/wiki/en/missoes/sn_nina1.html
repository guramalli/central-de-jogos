💭 The decorated surfboard

Level 15 Side quest Brazil

Given by: Nina, the surfer
is at Footvolley Beach — embaixo, on the left of the map (southwest)

The story

I want to decorate my surfboard with starfish (the ones opponents drop, the real ones stay in the sea!). Can you bring me 3?

What to do

🎯 Collect 3x Starfish (Beach)

  • Collect 3x Starfish

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Footvolley Player — Level 14 (drops the most Starfish)
🗺️Where: Footvolley Beach (Brazil)
🧭Path: from Campinho Village: Campinho Village → Footvolley Beach
📌Inside: em cima of the map (north)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 3,067 XP
  • 🪙 500 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

It looks great! Now I can even catch big waves.
