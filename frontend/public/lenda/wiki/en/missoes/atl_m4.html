The Elder Dragon

Level 290 Story Atlantis

Given by: Queen Marina, Atlantis coach
is at Atlantis — Sunken City — in the middle of the map

The story

The last portal holds the Elder Dragon, who has played soccer for a thousand years. Beat 30 and you'll be the legend of Atlantis!

What to do

  • Beat 30x Elder Dragon

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Elder Dragon — Level 296
🗺️Where: 🎯 Elder Dragon's Lair (Atlantis)
🧭Trip: 🌊 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to Captain Iara (at the port) and go down by submarine — you need the Diving Helmet.
🧭Path: from there: Atlantis → 🎯 Elder Dragon's Lair
📌Inside: on the left of the map (west)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 26,126,550 XP
  • 🪙 3,000,000 coins
  • 1x Dragon Crown
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

LEGEND OF ATLANTIS! The dragons bow when you walk by. And they say that above the clouds there's another field waiting...
