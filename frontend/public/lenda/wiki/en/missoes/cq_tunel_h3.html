👻 Offside!

Level 805 Forgotten Cup 🌀 Multiverse Stadium

Given by: Seu Saudade, the kit manager
is at 🏟️ Forgotten Stadium — on the left of the map (west)

The story

Dona Memória is always arguing about offside with the Linesman in the tunnel. She wants to see if you understand the rule. Go talk to her here in the lobby!

What to do

🎯 Answer Dona Memória’s questions (Forgotten Stadium lobby)

  • Talk to Dona Memória, the fan
  • There are 3 questions to answer (no cheating here!).

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

🙋Look for: Dona Memória, the fan
🗺️Where: 🏟️ Forgotten Stadium (🌀 Multiverse Stadium)
🧭Trip: 🌀 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to the Multiverse Guardian. 🏟️ In the Multiverse, go through the Forgotten Stadium Portal (level 700).
🧭Path: from there: 🌀 Multiverse Stadium → 🏟️ Forgotten Stadium
📌Inside: on the right of the map (east)
