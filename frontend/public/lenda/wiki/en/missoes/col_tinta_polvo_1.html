Collection: Octopus Ink

Level 255 Side quest Atlantis

Given by: Professor Coral, the collector
is at Atlantis — Sunken City — in the middle of the map

The story

My deep-sea collection needs 10× Octopus Ink. Who drops it: Juggler Octopus (Octopus Reef).

What to do

  • Collect 10x Octopus Ink

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Juggler Octopus — Level 258 (drops the most Octopus Ink)
🗺️Where: 🎯 Octopus Reef (Atlantis)
🧭Trip: 🌊 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to Captain Iara (at the port) and go down by submarine — you need the Diving Helmet.
🧭Path: from there: Atlantis → 🎯 Octopus Reef
📌Inside: em cima, on the left of the map (northwest)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 5,493,885 XP
  • 🪙 77,400 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

How lovely! My collection of Octopus Ink is growing. If you bring more, I have a big order!

➡️ Then unlocks: Big order: Octopus Ink
