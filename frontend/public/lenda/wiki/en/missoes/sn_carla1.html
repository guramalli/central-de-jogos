💭 The lost notes

Level 38 Side quest Brazil

Given by: Carla, the scout
is at Youth Training Center — on the left of the map (west)

The story

The wind blew my note clipboards all over the Training Center! The opponents here grabbed everything. Can you bring me 2 Scribbled Clipboards?

What to do

🎯 Collect 2x Scribbled Clipboards (Training Center)

  • Collect 2x Scribbled Clipboard

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Fitness Coach — Level 36 (drops the most Scribbled Clipboard)
🗺️Where: Youth Training Center (Brazil)
🧭Path: from Campinho Village: Campinho Village → Footvolley Beach → City → Youth Training Center
📌Inside: embaixo, on the right of the map (southeast)
