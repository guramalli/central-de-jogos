Costume: Dinosaur Costume

Level 8 Mount/look Brazil

Given by: Dona Vera, the fashion designer
is at City — Futsal Courts — embaixo, on the left of the map (southwest)

The story

I'm going to sew a DINOSAUR costume! I need a few little things from the Village and the beach.

What to do

  • Collect 20x Pigeon Feather
  • Collect 15x Pretty Seashell
  • Bring 400 coins

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Cheeky Pigeon — Level 3 (drops the most Pigeon Feather)
🎒Each item: Pigeon Feather: Cheeky Pigeon (Level 3, Campinho Village)
Pretty Seashell: Pinchy Crab (Level 10, Footvolley Beach)
🗺️Where: Campinho Village (Brazil)
📌Inside: em cima of the map (north)

Rewards
