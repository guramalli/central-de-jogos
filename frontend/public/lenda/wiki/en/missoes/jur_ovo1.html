🥚 An egg on the way

Level 566 Side quest 🌀 Multiverse Stadium

Given by: Grandpa Ptero, the egg hatcher
is at 🦖 Jurassic Valley — Camp — on the left of the map (west)

The story

The valley dinosaurs sometimes leave a Tricy egg along the way... it's super rare! But don't give up: after lots and lots of dinosaurs, an egg ALWAYS shows up. Bring it to me.

What to do

🎯 Find a Tricy Egg (dropped by the valley dinosaurs)

  • Collect 1x Tricy Egg

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Speedy Compy — Level 566 (drops the most Tricy Egg)
🗺️Where: 🦖 Jurassic Maze (🌀 Multiverse Stadium)
🧭Trip: 🌀 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to the Multiverse Guardian.
🧭Path: from there: 🌀 Multiverse Stadium → 🦖 Jurassic Valley → 🦖 Jurassic Maze
📌Inside: in the middle of the map, near the sign “🦖 FERN ROOM”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 49,437,026 XP
  • 🪙 1,132,000 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

What a beautiful egg! I'll put it in the incubator right now.

➡️ Then unlocks: 🥚 The incubator
