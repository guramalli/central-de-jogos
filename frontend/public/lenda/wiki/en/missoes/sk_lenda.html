Collection: Eternal Legend

Level 170 Mount/look Brazil

Given by: Dona Vera, the fashion designer
is at City — Futsal Courts — embaixo, on the left of the map (southwest)

The story

My masterpiece! Only for those who have beaten every arena, many times over.

What to do

  • Collect 2x Dirt Pitch Arena Trophy
  • Collect 2x Wave Arena Trophy
  • Collect 2x Pyramid Arena Trophy
  • Collect 2x Neon Sakura Arena Trophy
  • Collect 2x Blizzard Arena Trophy
  • Collect 2x Arena of Legends Trophy
  • Collect 100x Gold Thread

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Trovão, King of the Dirt Pitch boss — Level 32 (drops the most Dirt Pitch Arena Trophy)
🎒Each item: Dirt Pitch Arena Trophy: Trovão (Level 32, boss, Dirt Pitch Arena)
Wave Arena Trophy: The Wave Queen (Level 44, boss, Wave Arena)
Pyramid Arena Trophy: The Golden Sphinx (Level 66, boss, Pyramid Arena)
Neon Sakura Arena Trophy: Neon Ronin (Level 90, boss, Neon Sakura Arena)
Blizzard Arena Trophy: The Blizzard Queen (Level 164, boss, Blizzard Arena)
Arena of Legends Trophy: The Immortal (Level 194, boss, Arena of Legends)
Gold Thread: Maestro Pivot (Level 170, Madrid)
🗺️Where: Dirt Pitch Arena (Brazil)
🧭Path: from Campinho Village: Campinho Village → Footvolley Beach → City → Dirt Pitch Arena

Rewards

  • ⭐ 2,050,754 XP
  • 👕 New look: lenda
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

You're ETERNAL! Put it on with the ✨ Look button.
