💭 Samba circle on the sand

Level 122 Side quest Brazil

Given by: Nina, the surfer
is at Footvolley Beach — embaixo, on the left of the map (southwest)

The story

In Rio there’s Copacabana Beach, with its wavy sidewalk! Can you bring me 3 Little Tambourines from there? I’ll start a samba circle here on the beach!

What to do

🎯 Collect 3x Little Tambourines (Rio de Janeiro)

  • Collect 3x Little Tambourine

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Copacabana Samba Dancer — Level 118 (drops the most Little Tambourine)
🗺️Where: Rio de Janeiro — Copacabana (Brazil)
🧭Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to Rio de Janeiro, Brazil (from level 112) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: embaixo, on the left of the map (southwest), near the sign “🏖️ COPACABANA”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 739,062 XP
  • 🪙 36,600 coins
  • 2x Sticker Pack
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

Boom-boom-tah! Now the beach has samba and footvolley. Thanks, star!

⬅️ First, finish: 💭 Surfing in Miami
