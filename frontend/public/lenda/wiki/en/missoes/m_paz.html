Peace Mission: Rival Neighborhood

Level 166 Story Madrid

Given by: Carmen, neighborhood teacher
is at Madrid — Plaza and Barrio — in the middle of the map

The story

The rowdy fans of the Rival Neighborhood bully everyone who walks by. Beat 30 by playing fair and show that rivalry belongs on the field.

What to do

  • Beat 30x Rival Neighborhood Rowdy Fan

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Rival Neighborhood Rowdy Fan — Level 170
🗺️Where: Madrid — Plaza and Barrio
🧭Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to Madrid, Spain (from level 162) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: em cima, on the left of the map (northwest)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 1,906,442 XP
  • 🪙 31,695 coins
  • 1x Megaphone

Today they sang instead of booing. That's how you cheer!
