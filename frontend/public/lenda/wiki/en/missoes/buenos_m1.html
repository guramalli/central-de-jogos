Welcome to Buenos Aires

Level 100 Story Buenos Aires

Given by: Doña Rosa from the Empanada Stand
is at Buenos Aires — La Boca — em cima, on the left of the map (northwest)

The story

The Caminito Pibe opponents run through the streets of Buenos Aires. Show them Brazilian soccer: get past 30 of them.

What to do

  • Beat 30x Caminito Pibe

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Caminito Pibe — Level 106
🗺️Where: Buenos Aires — La Boca
🧭Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to Buenos Aires, Argentina (from level 100) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: on the right of the map (east), near the sign “🎨 CAMINITO”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 398,986 XP
  • 🪙 12,720 coins
  • 5x Empanadas
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

Buenos Aires already knows your name!
