🐕 A home for Pipoca

Level 14 Side quest Brazil

Given by: Aunt Zuzu from the Snack Bar
is at Campinho Village — in the middle of the map

The story

Tonhão keeps kicking the ball at the poor little guy! Teach him a lesson (beat Tonhão 3 times) and the puppy is yours. I already gave him a name: Pipoca!

What to do

  • Beat 3x Tonhão, Rival Captain

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Tonhão, Rival Captain boss — Level 11
🗺️Where: Campinho Village (Brazil)
📌Inside: in the middle of the map

Rewards

  • ⭐ 2,633 XP
  • 🪙 4,000 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

Pipoca is now your PET! Bring him along in Equipment → ✨ Cosmetics. Hunting together, he grows and brings extra coins. Take good care of him, okay?

⬅️ First, finish: 🐕 The popcorn puppy
