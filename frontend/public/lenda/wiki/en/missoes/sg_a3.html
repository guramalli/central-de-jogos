📜 Waiting for Granny to wake up

Level 685 Origin Saga Atlantis

Given by: Professor Coral, the collector
is at Atlantis — Sunken City — in the middle of the map

The story

A 900-year-old turtle sleeps a lot! She'll wake up in about 2 hours. Come back later.

What to do

🎯 Wait 2 h (real time)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 140,671,586 XP
  • 🪙 3,600,000 coins
  • 1x Ocean Panel

Hee-hee-hee... ACHOO! Granny Tuga sneezed out the panel and said sorry for the mix-up. The 5th panel is yours! Now the map is glowing inside a MOUNTAIN: Stonehold, the kingdom of the dwarves.
