🎟️ The yellowed ticket

Level 700 Forgotten Cup 🌀 Multiverse Stadium

Given by: Grand Guardian Orbitto
is at 🌀 Multiverse Stadium — embaixo of the map (south)

The story

Look what I found on the stadium floor: an old, yellowed ticket for the "Origin Cup"! The sealed portal lit up. Go through it and talk to Seu Saudade inside.

I know the oldest legends in the universe, but I’ve never heard of this cup! The portal that was sealed, at the top of the Multiverse Stadium, now leads to a very old stadium. The ticket is your way in.

What to do

🎯 Go through the Forgotten Portal (Multiverse Stadium) and talk to Seu Saudade

  • Talk to Seu Saudade, the kit manager

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

🗺️Where: 🏟️ Forgotten Stadium (🌀 Multiverse Stadium)
🧭Trip: 🌀 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to the Multiverse Guardian. 🏟️ In the Multiverse, go through the Forgotten Stadium Portal (level 700).
🧭Path: from there: 🌀 Multiverse Stadium → 🏟️ Forgotten Stadium
📌Inside: on the left of the map (west)
