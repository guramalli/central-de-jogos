Skate Park Hangout

Level 18 Story Brazil

Given by: Master Ginga
is at City — Futsal Courts — embaixo, on the right of the map (southeast)

The story

In the city, the game is fast. The cheeky skaters are getting in the way on the court. Dribble past 15 of them.

What to do

  • Beat 15x Cheeky Skater

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Cheeky Skater — Level 20
🗺️Where: City — Futsal Courts (Brazil)
🧭Path: from Campinho Village: Campinho Village → Footvolley Beach → City
📌Inside: em cima, on the left of the map (northwest)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 5,000 XP
  • 🪙 400 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

The court is clear! (The ELASTICO arrives on its own at level 20: this way, that way... and you're through.)

➡️ Then unlocks: Court Control
