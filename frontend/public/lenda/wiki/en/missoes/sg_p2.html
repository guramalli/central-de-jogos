📜 Worthy of the legend

Level 420 Story 🌀 Multiverse Stadium

Given by: Grand Guardian Orbitto
is at 🌀 Multiverse Stadium — embaixo of the map (south)

The story

The 7 panels only show themselves to someone with a champion's heart. Prove you're worthy: beat the Nebula Emperor 3 times. Don't worry, he's a good loser.

What to do

🎯 Beat 3x Nebula Emperor

  • Beat 3x Nebula Emperor

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Nebula Emperor boss — Level 398
🗺️Where: Orion Nebula — Star Field
🧭Trip: 🚀 Go to the Space Station (in Rio, with Dr. Estela — you need the Astronaut Suit), and from there the Control Tower takes you to Orion Nebula.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: em cima, on the left of the map (northwest)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 48,164,880 XP
  • 🪙 5,700,000 coins
  • 5x Multiverse Elixir
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

Well done! Look at the map: it's glowing over the MOON. That's where the first panel is. Talk to Commander Luna.
