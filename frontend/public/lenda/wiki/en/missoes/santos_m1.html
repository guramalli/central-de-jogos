Welcome to Santos

Level 182 Story Santos

Given by: Seu Nenê the Pastel Maker
is at Santos — Waterfront and Vila Belmiro — in the middle of the map

The story

The Meninos da Vila run along the whole seafront with step-overs and rainbow flicks. Show them how Campinho Village plays: get past 30 Village Rascals.

What to do

  • Beat 30x Village Kid

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Village Kid — Level 188
🗺️Where: Santos — Waterfront and Vila Belmiro
🧭Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to Santos, Brazil (Vila Belmiro) (from level 182) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: embaixo, on the left of the map (southwest)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 2,526,996 XP
  • 🪙 22,560 coins
  • 5x Pastel with Sugarcane Juice
