Warm-Up

Level 1 Story Brazil

Given by: Seu Zé
is at Campinho Village — embaixo, on the left of the map (southwest)

The story

So you want to play ball? Warm up first. These cheeky pigeons are always sitting on the little pitch. Dribble past 6 of them!

What to do

  • Beat 6x Cheeky Pigeon

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Cheeky Pigeon — Level 3
🗺️Where: Campinho Village (Brazil)
📌Inside: em cima of the map (north)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 60 XP
  • 🪙 20 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

Nice! You warmed up well. Dribbles come with experience: with every new level you learn them on your own (the STEP-OVER arrives at level 2 — use it on the hotbar when you're right next to an opponent).

⬅️ First, finish: My first ball

➡️ Then unlocks: The Ball Hogs
