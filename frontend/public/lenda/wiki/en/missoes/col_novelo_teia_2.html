Big order: Ball of Web

Level 219 Side quest Atlantis

Given by: Professor Coral, the collector
is at Atlantis — Sunken City — in the middle of the map

The story

I'm building a museum in Atlantis and I'm missing 25× Ball of Web — it’s a big order, but the prize is worth it! Who drops it: Goalkeeper Spider (Goalkeeper Spider's Cellar).

What to do

  • Collect 25x Ball of Web

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Goalkeeper Spider — Level 222 (drops the most Ball of Web)
🗺️Where: 🎯 Goalkeeper Spider's Cellar (Atlantis)
🧭Trip: 🌊 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to Captain Iara (at the port) and go down by submarine — you need the Diving Helmet.
🧭Path: from there: Atlantis → 🎯 Goalkeeper Spider's Cellar
📌Inside: em cima of the map (north)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 6,670,574 XP
  • 🪙 177,600 coins
  • 4x Gold Thread
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

Amazing! 25× Ball of Web! Nobody has ever brought me this many. Take these gold threads to the Forge!
