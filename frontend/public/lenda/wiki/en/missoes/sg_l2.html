📜 The Moon Detector

Level 435 Origin Saga Moon

Given by: Commander Luna
is at Moon — Sea of Tranquility — in the middle of the map

The story

The detector runs on super-fine Moon Dust. Bring 60 Moon Dust from Moon hunts (the creatures there drop it).

What to do

🎯 Collect 60 Moon Dust (Moon hunts)

  • Collect 60x Moon Dust

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Selenite Goalie — Level 320 (drops the most Moon Dust)
🗺️Where: Moon — Sea of Tranquility
🧭Trip: 🚀 Go to the Space Station (in Rio, with Dr. Estela — you need the Astronaut Suit), and from there the Control Tower takes you to Moon.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: embaixo, on the left of the map (southwest), near the sign “🎯 HUNTING AREA: Selenite Stadium”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 66,948,288 XP
  • 🪙 2,200,000 coins
  • 1x Moon Detector
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

Done! Now it needs to charge with Earthlight.

⬅️ First, finish: 📜 To the Moon

➡️ Then unlocks: 📜 Charging with Earthlight
