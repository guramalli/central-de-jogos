Challenge in Saturn

Level 353 Side quest Saturn

Given by: Astronomer Vega
is at Saturn — Crystal Rings — in the middle of the map

The story

The "Little Floating Ring" team wants to test their strength against a human. Get past 60 of them!

What to do

  • Beat 60x Little Floating Ring

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Little Floating Ring — Level 357
🗺️Where: Saturn — Crystal Rings
🧭Trip: 🚀 Go to the Space Station (in Rio, with Dr. Estela — you need the Astronaut Suit), and from there the Control Tower takes you to Saturn.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: on the right of the map (east), near the sign “🎯 HUNTING AREA: Ring Carousel”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 28,458,840 XP
  • 🪙 1,071,000 coins
  • 1x Star Jersey
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

They asked for a rematch... but they already know who's boss!

➡️ Then unlocks: The best team of Saturn
