The story

Wall Football Club never let in a goal... and never scored one! Beat 50 Wall Defenders in the Players’ Tunnel and teach them the joy of attacking.

Wall FC tied every game 0-0. They defended so well that they forgot to try to score! Tip: the Wall Defender only goes down to strong plays.
