Bossy Lifeguards

Level 15 Story Brazil

Given by: Chief Marinho
is at Footvolley Beach — em cima, on the left of the map (northwest)

The story

Some lifeguards think they own the beach. Put 12 of them in their place.

What to do

  • Beat 12x Bossy Lifeguard

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Bossy Lifeguard — Level 18
🗺️Where: Footvolley Beach (Brazil)
🧭Path: from Campinho Village: Campinho Village → Footvolley Beach
📌Inside: embaixo of the map (south)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 3,200 XP
  • 🪙 260 coins
  • 1x Plastic Shin Guard
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

The beach is peaceful again. Take this shin guard!

⬅️ First, finish: Thief Seagulls
