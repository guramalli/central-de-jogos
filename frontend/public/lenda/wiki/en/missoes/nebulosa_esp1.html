Challenge in the Nebula

Level 378 Side quest Orion Nebula

Given by: Guardian Orion
is at Orion Nebula — Star Field — in the middle of the map

The story

The "Little Nebula Cloud" team wants to test their strength against a human. Get past 60 of them!

What to do

  • Beat 60x Little Nebula Cloud

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Little Nebula Cloud — Level 382
🗺️Where: Orion Nebula — Star Field
🧭Trip: 🚀 Go to the Space Station (in Rio, with Dr. Estela — you need the Astronaut Suit), and from there the Control Tower takes you to Orion Nebula.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: on the left of the map (west), near the sign “🎯 HUNTING AREA: Sea of Clouds”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 35,013,490 XP
  • 🪙 1,146,000 coins
  • 1x Galactic Jersey
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

They asked for a rematch... but they already know who's boss!

➡️ Then unlocks: The best team of the Nebula
