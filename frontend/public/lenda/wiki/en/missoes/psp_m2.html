🛂 Stamps on the road

Level 20 Side quest Brazil

Given by: Dona Zuleide, from the Travel Agency
is at Campinho Village — on the right of the map (east)

The story

Take the passport on the road! Master Tatá, at Footvolley Beach, and Master Ginga, at the City Futsal Courts, also ask questions and give stamps. Collect 3 stamps and come back here to the Travel Agency.

What to do

🎯 Have 3 stamps in the Passport (Beach: Master Tatá · City: Master Ginga)

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

🙋Look for: Master Tatá (asks the 3 Passport questions)
🗺️Where: Footvolley Beach (Brazil)
🧭Path: from Campinho Village: Campinho Village → Footvolley Beach
📌Inside: em cima of the map (north)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 1,500 XP
  • 🪙 250 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

3 stamps! When you fly around the world, every city has its own. With 5 stamps, you earn the title Savvy Traveler!

⬅️ First, finish: 🛂 The Star's Passport
