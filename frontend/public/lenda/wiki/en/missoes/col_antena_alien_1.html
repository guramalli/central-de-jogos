Collection: Alien Antenna

Level 302 Side quest Moon

Given by: Commander Luna
is at Moon — Sea of Tranquility — in the middle of the map

The story

The Moon scientists want to study 10× Alien Antenna. Who drops it: Moon Bunny (Bunny Craters).

What to do

  • Collect 10x Alien Antenna

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Moon Bunny — Level 305 (drops the most Alien Antenna)
🗺️Where: Moon — Sea of Tranquility
🧭Trip: 🚀 Go to the Space Station (in Rio, with Dr. Estela — you need the Astronaut Suit), and from there the Control Tower takes you to Moon.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: em cima, on the right of the map (northeast), near the sign “🎯 HUNTING AREA: Bunny Craters”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 9,134,740 XP
  • 🪙 91,500 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

How lovely! My collection of Alien Antenna is growing. If you bring more, I have a big order!

➡️ Then unlocks: Big order: Alien Antenna
