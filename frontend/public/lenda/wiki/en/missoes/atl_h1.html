🌊 The statue in the square

Level 206 Story Atlantis

Given by: Queen Marina, Atlantis coach
is at Atlantis — Sunken City — in the middle of the map

The story

The statue of Neptune, here in the square, holds a stone ball with seven panels. Professor Coral has studied this statue for years. Go talk to him!

What to do

🎯 Talk to Professor Coral (Atlantis square)

  • Talk to Professor Coral, the collector

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

🙋Look for: Professor Coral, the collector
🗺️Where: Atlantis — Sunken City
🧭Trip: 🌊 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to Captain Iara (at the port) and go down by submarine — you need the Diving Helmet.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: in the middle of the map

Rewards

  • 🪙 160,000 coins
  • 5x Stamina Bottle Supreme
