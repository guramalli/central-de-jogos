🏆 The King of the Stands

Level 895 Forgotten Cup 🌀 Multiverse Stadium

Given by: Seu Saudade, the kit manager
is at 🏟️ Forgotten Stadium — on the left of the map (west)

The story

The King of the Stands leads the fans’ party. Beat the King in the VIP box, up at the top of the Infinite Stands, and he’ll start a chant with your name!

He was the very first fan of the Origin Cup and never missed a game... as a spectator! When the Thunder Drum plays, the wave pushes you: stand firm.

What to do

🎯 Beat 1x King of the Stands (Infinite Stands)

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: King of the Stands boss — Level 903
🗺️Where: 📣 Infinite Stands (🌀 Multiverse Stadium)
🧭Trip: 🌀 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to the Multiverse Guardian. 🏟️ In the Multiverse, go through the Forgotten Stadium Portal (level 700).
🧭Path: from there: 🌀 Multiverse Stadium → 🏟️ Forgotten Stadium → 📣 Infinite Stands
📌Inside: em cima of the map (north)
