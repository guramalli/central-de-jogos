Collection: Golden Star

Level 45 Mount/look Brazil

Given by: Dona Vera, the fashion designer
is at City — Futsal Courts — embaixo, on the left of the map (southwest)

The story

A golden star uniform! Only with super rare items.

What to do

  • Collect 12x Red Card
  • Collect 20x Goalkeeper Glove
  • Collect 8x Gold Thread

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Strict Referee — Level 52 (drops the most Red Card)
🎒Each item: Red Card: Strict Referee (Level 52, Legendary Stadium)
Goalkeeper Glove: Sweeper Keeper (Level 25, City)
Gold Thread: Playmaking Midfielder (Level 45, Legendary Stadium)
🗺️Where: Legendary Stadium (Brazil)
🧭Path: from Campinho Village: Campinho Village → Footvolley Beach → City → Youth Training Center → Legendary Stadium
📌Inside: in the middle of the map

Rewards

  • ⭐ 37,880 XP
  • 👕 New look: ouro
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

It shines brighter than the sun! Put it on with the ✨ Look button.

⬅️ First, finish: Collection: Camo Star

➡️ Then unlocks: Collection: Dribble Pharaoh
