📜 The lucky charm

Level 480 Origin Saga Mars

Given by: Engineer Rubi
is at Mars — Red Valley — in the middle of the map

The story

The General will only give it back if he loses in a way he'll never forget. Beat the Martian General 10 times: after the tenth, he'll admit the charm isn't bringing him any luck at all!

What to do

🎯 Beat 10x Martian General

  • Beat 10x Martian General

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Martian General boss — Level 353
🗺️Where: Mars — Red Valley
🧭Trip: 🚀 Go to the Space Station (in Rio, with Dr. Estela — you need the Astronaut Suit), and from there the Control Tower takes you to Mars.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: embaixo of the map (south)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 96,165,307 XP
  • 🪙 7,300,000 coins
  • 1x Tower Chest
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

"Okay, okay! This charm is useless!" — the General tossed the panel to you. But it fell into a hole in the Rover Desert...
