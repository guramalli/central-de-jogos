Training in Munich

Level 150 Story Munich

Given by: Coach Hans
is at Munich — Clock Square — in the middle of the map

The story

The Square Clockmaker opponents read the game like nobody else. Beat 30 of them in the streets of Munich to learn.

What to do

  • Beat 30x Square Clockmaker

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Square Clockmaker — Level 154
🗺️Where: Munich — Clock Square

📌Inside: on the left of the map (west), near the sign “🥨 VIKTUALIENMARKT”
