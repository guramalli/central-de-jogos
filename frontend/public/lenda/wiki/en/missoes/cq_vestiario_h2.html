👻 The ball that ran away

Level 725 Forgotten Cup 🌀 Multiverse Stadium

Given by: Dona Memória, the fan
is at 🏟️ Forgotten Stadium — on the right of the map (east)

The story

The Lightning Ball Boy grabbed Sandlot Lightning’s Signed Ball and ran off! He lives in the Abandoned Locker Room. Bring the ball back.

Sandlot Lightning was the fastest team in the Cup: they ran so much they forgot to pass the ball! The Ball Boy is quick and runs off with the ball: mark him and don’t let him get away.

What to do

🎯 Get the Signed Ball back from the Lightning Ball Boy (Abandoned Locker Room)

  • Collect 1x Signed Ball (Lightning Ball Kid)

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Lightning Ball Kid — Level 740 (drops the most Signed Ball)
🗺️Where: 👕 Abandoned Locker Room (🌀 Multiverse Stadium)
🧭Trip: 🌀 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to the Multiverse Guardian. 🏟️ In the Multiverse, go through the Forgotten Stadium Portal (level 700).
🧭Path: from there: 🌀 Multiverse Stadium → 🏟️ Forgotten Stadium → 👕 Abandoned Locker Room
📌Inside: on the left of the map (west)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 208,654,983 XP
  • 🪙 2,175,000 coins
  • 4x Forgotten Ticket
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

How wonderful! Each signature belongs to a Sandlot Lightning player. By the end of the Cup they had learned to pass the ball... too bad the final stopped.

⬅️ First, finish: 👻 The team that never left the bench

➡️ Then unlocks: 👻 The rule book
