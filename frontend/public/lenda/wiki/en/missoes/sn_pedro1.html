💭 The ticket collection

Level 48 Side quest Brazil

Given by: Pedrinho, young fan
is at Legendary Stadium — embaixo, on the right of the map (southeast)

The story

I keep a ticket from every game! The stadium opponents keep dropping crumpled ones. Can you bring me 8 Crumpled Tickets?

What to do

🎯 Collect 8x Crumpled Tickets (Stadium)

  • Collect 8x Crumpled Ticket

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

🗺️Where: Legendary Stadium (Brazil)
🧭Path: from Campinho Village: Campinho Village → Footvolley Beach → City → Youth Training Center → Legendary Stadium
📌Inside: em cima, on the left of the map (northwest)
