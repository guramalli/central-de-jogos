The Astronaut Suit

Level 290 Story Brazil

Given by: Dr. Estela, the astronaut
is at Rio de Janeiro — Copacabana — on the left of the map (west)

The story

There's no air in space! To take off with me, bring 20 Tufts of Yeti Fur, 10 Ghost Sheets, 5 Dragon Scales and 150,000 coins: I'll sew you an Astronaut Suit.

Space is freezing cold. The yeti fur is the insulation, the ghost sheet is the light fabric and the dragon scales protect you from the heat of takeoff. All of it drops in the Atlantis portals.

What to do

  • Collect 20x Tuft of Yeti Fur
  • Collect 10x Ghost Sheet
  • Collect 5x Dragon Scale
  • Bring 150,000 coins

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Big Defender Yeti — Level 276 (drops the most Tuft of Yeti Fur)
🎒Each item: Tuft of Yeti Fur: Big Defender Yeti (Level 276, 🎯 Yeti Cave)
Ghost Sheet: Little Ghost Fan (Level 282, 🎯 Ghost Stadium)
Dragon Scale: Little Striker Dragon (Level 288, 🎯 Dragon Volcano)
🗺️Where: 🎯 Yeti Cave (Atlantis)
🧭Trip: 🌊 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to Captain Iara (at the port) and go down by submarine — you need the Diving Helmet.
🧭Path: from there: Atlantis → 🎯 Yeti Cave
📌Inside: in the middle of the map

Rewards

  • ⭐ 7,837,965 XP
  • 1x Astronaut Suit
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

A perfect fit! With this suit you can handle the cold and the vacuum. The aliens had better get ready!
