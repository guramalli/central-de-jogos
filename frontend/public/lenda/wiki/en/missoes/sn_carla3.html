💭 Talent in Madrid

Level 176 Side quest Brazil

Given by: Carla, the scout
is at Youth Training Center — on the left of the map (west)

The story

The clubs in Madrid asked to see you play! Bring 2 Flamenco Fans from there so I can send them as gifts to the directors. It’s tradition!

What to do

🎯 Collect 2x Flamenco Fans (Madrid)

  • Collect 2x Flamenco Fan

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Speedy Flamenco — Level 170 (drops the most Flamenco Fan)
🗺️Where: Madrid — Plaza and Barrio
🧭Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to Madrid, Spain (from level 162) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: embaixo of the map (south), near the sign “🚣 PARQUE DEL RETIRO”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 2,280,594 XP
  • 🪙 52,800 coins
  • 2x Sticker Pack

The directors loved them! Now you're famous all over Europe.
