🐱 Sortudo, the Lucky Kitty

Level 76 Side quest Tokyo

Given by: Dona Yuki from the Onigiri Stand
is at Tokyo — Neon District — on the left of the map (west)

The story

He was seen near the Dribble Sensei, but the Sensei won't let anyone get close! Beat the Sensei 3 times and bring the kitty back.

What to do

  • Beat 3x The Dribble Sensei

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: The Dribble Sensei boss — Level 84
🗺️Where: Tokyo — Neon District
🧭Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to Tokyo, Japan (from level 74) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: embaixo, on the right of the map (southeast), near the sign “⛩️ SENSEI'S TEMPLE”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 211,327 XP
  • 🪙 30,400 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

He doesn't want to leave your side! Keep him: his name is SORTUDO. Bring him along in Equipment → ✨ Cosmetics — with him, opponents drop more items!
