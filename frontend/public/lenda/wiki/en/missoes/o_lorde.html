The Ballon d’Or

Level 190 Story London

Given by: Oliver, community coach
is at London — Camden and East End — em cima of the map (north)

The story

THE LORD OF FOOTBALL keeps the Ballon d’Or at Wembley Field, on the south bank of the Thames. Only a legend can take it from him.

What to do

  • Beat 1x The Lord of Soccer

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: The Lord of Soccer boss — Level 190
🗺️Where: London — Camden and East End
🧭Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to London, England (from level 176) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: embaixo of the map (south), near the sign “🏆 WEMBLEY FIELD”

Rewards
