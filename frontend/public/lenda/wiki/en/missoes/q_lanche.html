Champion's Snack

Level 2 Side quest Brazil

Given by: Aunt Zuzu from the Snack Bar
is at Campinho Village — in the middle of the map

The story

You can't train on an empty stomach! Eat 3 foods (you can buy them here from me). Each food gives a bonus for a few minutes. To eat: in the 🎒 Backpacks panel (or the I key), click the food and then "Eat" (or right-click it).

What to do

🎯 Eat 3 foods: click the food in the 🎒 Backpack and then Eat

Rewards

  • ⭐ 90 XP
  • 5x Cheese Bread
  • 2x Coxinha
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

That's it! Good food = strong star. Take some cheese bread for the road.
