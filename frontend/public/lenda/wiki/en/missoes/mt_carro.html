My first car

Level 50 Mount/look Brazil

Given by: Dona Rita from the Garage
is at City — Futsal Courts — embaixo, on the left of the map (southwest)

The story

You're a grown-up now! I have a lightly used little car. Bring what I need plus the payment.

What to do

  • Collect 12x Stopwatch
  • Collect 12x Tactics Clipboard
  • Collect 10x Yellow Card
  • Bring 40,000 coins

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Fitness Coach — Level 36 (drops the most Stopwatch)
🎒Each item: Stopwatch: Fitness Coach (Level 36, Youth Training Center)
Tactics Clipboard: Playmaking Midfielder (Level 45, Legendary Stadium)
Yellow Card: Strict Referee (Level 52, Legendary Stadium)
🗺️Where: Youth Training Center (Brazil)
🧭Path: from Campinho Village: Campinho Village → Footvolley Beach → City → Youth Training Center
📌Inside: embaixo, on the right of the map (southeast)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 66,730 XP
  • 🛹 Mount: Compact Car
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

Keys in hand! Drive carefully, okay?

⬅️ First, finish: The blue scooter
