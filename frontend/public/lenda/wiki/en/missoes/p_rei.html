The Sand King

Level 17 Story Brazil

Given by: Master Tatá
is at Footvolley Beach — em cima of the map (north)

The story

The SAND KING lives at the end of the beach, past the coconut trees to the south. He's never lost. Until today.

What to do

  • Beat 1x Sand King

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Sand King boss — Level 21
🗺️Where: Footvolley Beach (Brazil)
🧭Path: from Campinho Village: Campinho Village → Footvolley Beach
📌Inside: embaixo of the map (south), near the sign “🐢 SEA TURTLE NEST”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 6,500 XP
  • 🪙 500 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

LEGENDARY! The road to the CITY (north of the beach) is open.
