🌊 The map of the currents

Level 280 Story Atlantis

Given by: Nina the Mermaid
is at 🎯 Octopus Reef — embaixo of the map (south)

The story

I’ll draw where Granny Tuga sleeps, so you never forget. Bring 8 Octopus Inks: the Juggler Octopuses here at the Reef drop them.

What to do

🎯 Collect 8 Octopus Inks (Octopus Reef)

  • Collect 8x Octopus Ink

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Juggler Octopus — Level 258 (drops the most Octopus Ink)
🗺️Where: 🎯 Octopus Reef (Atlantis)
🧭Trip: 🌊 In Rio de Janeiro, talk to Captain Iara (at the port) and go down by submarine — you need the Diving Helmet.
🧭Path: from there: Atlantis → 🎯 Octopus Reef
📌Inside: em cima, on the left of the map (northwest)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 11,742,483 XP
  • 🪙 260,000 coins
  • 10x Stamina Bottle Supreme
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

There, the reef map! Keep this secret: when you’re a legend of the universe, come back and talk to Professor Coral. Granny Tuga will wait.

⬅️ First, finish: 🌊 Glowing bubbles
