💭 Surfing in Miami

Level 88 Side quest Brazil

Given by: Nina, the surfer
is at Footvolley Beach — embaixo, on the left of the map (southwest)

The story

MIAMI has colorful lifeguard towers just like ours! If you go there, can you bring me a Mini Surfboard? It's so I can practice at home, hehe.

What to do

🎯 Collect 1x Mini Surfboard (Miami)

  • Collect 1x Mini Surfboard

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Speedy Surfer — Level 93 (drops the most Mini Surfboard)
🗺️Where: Miami — Ocean Drive
🧭Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to Miami, United States (from level 86) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: em cima, on the right of the map (northeast), near the sign “🏄 NEON BOARDWALK”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 368,225 XP
  • 🪙 26,400 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

A surfboard from Miami! I'll put it on my shelf, right next to my footvolley medal.

⬅️ First, finish: 💭 The decorated surfboard

➡️ Then unlocks: 💭 Samba circle on the sand
