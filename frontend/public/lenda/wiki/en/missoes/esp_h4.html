🚀 The rover's radio

Level 344 Story Mars

Given by: Engineer Rubi
is at Mars — Red Valley — in the middle of the map

The story

An old rover recorded a mysterious signal, but its radio broke. Bring me 6 Robot Gears (Mining Robots drop them) and I'll fix it.

What to do

🎯 Collect 6 Robot Gears (Mars hunts)

  • Collect 6x Robot Gear

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Mining Robot — Level 340 (drops the most Robot Gear)
🗺️Where: Mars — Red Valley
🧭Trip: 🚀 Go to the Space Station (in Rio, with Dr. Estela — you need the Astronaut Suit), and from there the Control Tower takes you to Mars.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: on the left of the map (west), near the sign “🎯 HUNTING AREA: Robot Mine”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 21,930,270 XP
  • 🪙 600,000 coins
  • 5x Focus Drink Legendary
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

There, listen: beep... beep... beep... SEVEN blinks, and then silence. This signal comes from far, far away!

⬅️ First, finish: 🚀 The General’s medal

➡️ Then unlocks: 🚀 The glow in the ice
