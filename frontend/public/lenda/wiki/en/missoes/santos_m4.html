Captain of the Meninos da Vila

Level 190 Story Santos

Given by: Dona Zilda, youth team coach
is at Santos — Waterfront and Vila Belmiro — in the middle of the map

The story

The CAPTAIN OF THE MENINOS DA VILA rules Beach Point. Beat him and all of Vila Belmiro will be talking about you!

What to do

  • Beat 1x Captain of the Meninos da Vila

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Captain of the Meninos da Vila boss — Level 192
🗺️Where: Santos — Waterfront and Vila Belmiro
🧭Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to Santos, Brazil (Vila Belmiro) (from level 182) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: embaixo, on the right of the map (southeast), near the sign “CAPTAIN OF THE MENINOS DA VILA's turf”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 5,044,001 XP
  • 🪙 75,200 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

The Meninos da Vila gave you a round of applause! Now go to the Pelé Museum: the guide has a story to tell you.
