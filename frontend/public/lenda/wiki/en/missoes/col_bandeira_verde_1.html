Collection: Green-and-Yellow Flag

Level 115 Side quest Brazil

Given by: Dona Glória, national team coach
is at Rio de Janeiro — Copacabana — in the middle of the map

The story

The samba school wants 10× Green-and-Yellow Flag. Who drops it: Geral Fan.

What to do

  • Collect 10x Green-and-Yellow Flag

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Geral Fan — Level 118 (drops the most Green-and-Yellow Flag)
🗺️Where: Rio de Janeiro — Copacabana (Brazil)
🧭Trip: ✈️ Take the plane to Rio de Janeiro, Brazil (from level 112) — in Brazil, the airport is in the City, with Flight Attendant Luana.
🧭Path: the trip takes you right there.
📌Inside: em cima, on the right of the map (northeast)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 500,015 XP
  • 🪙 35,400 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

How lovely! My collection of Green-and-Yellow Flag is growing. If you bring more, I have a big order!

➡️ Then unlocks: Big order: Green-and-Yellow Flag
