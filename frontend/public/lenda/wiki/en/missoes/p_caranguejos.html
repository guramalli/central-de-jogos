Feet in the Sand

Level 10 Story Brazil

Given by: Master Tatá
is at Footvolley Beach — em cima of the map (north)

The story

Sand is a whole different sport, buddy. Learn to move by dribbling past 12 pinchy crabs.

What to do

  • Beat 12x Pinchy Crab

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Pinchy Crab — Level 10
🗺️Where: Footvolley Beach (Brazil)
🧭Path: from Campinho Village: Campinho Village → Footvolley Beach
📌Inside: em cima, on the right of the map (northeast), near the sign “🚣 FISHERMEN'S PIER”

Rewards

  • ⭐ 900 XP
  • 🪙 120 coins
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

Great job on the sand! (The RAINBOW FLICK arrives on its own at level 12: the ball goes over and the defender is left staring at the sky!)

➡️ Then unlocks: Footvolley King
