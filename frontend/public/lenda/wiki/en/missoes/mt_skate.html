My first skateboard

Level 5 Mount/look Brazil

Given by: Seu Pedal, from the bike shop
is at Campinho Village — em cima of the map (north)

The story

I've got an old skateboard sitting here in the shop! Bring me a few things so I can fix it up, plus some coins for the new part.

What to do

  • Collect 5x Flat Ball
  • Collect 15x Pigeon Feather
  • Bring 100 coins

📍 Where to find it and how to get there

👾Who: Ball-Hog Kid — Level 5 (drops the most Flat Ball)
🎒Each item: Flat Ball: Ball-Hog Kid (Level 5, Campinho Village)
Pigeon Feather: Cheeky Pigeon (Level 3, Campinho Village)
🗺️Where: Campinho Village (Brazil)
📌Inside: embaixo of the map (south)

Rewards

  • ⭐ 300 XP
  • 🛹 Mount: Skateboard
When you finish (there may be a surprise!)

All set! Press P to hop on and off the skateboard. If you go to dribble someone, you'll hop off by yourself.

➡️ Then unlocks: Bike with a basket
